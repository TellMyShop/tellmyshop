# Changelog

Short summary of each version. Full notes with what you need to do: [tellmyshop.com/docs/changelog](https://tellmyshop.com/docs/changelog?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=linki-zewnetrzne). PrestaShop and WordPress share the same version numbers.

## 3.2.0 · 2026-11-05

- Big jobs in one approval (Pro): change hundreds of products by a rule, find and replace in content, several steps as one package. Large jobs run in the background and can be undone in one step.
- New ready-made jobs: new collection, products without sales, retiring a product, out-of-stock products, catalogue clean-up and more.
- Product feeds for Google, Meta, Ceneo and CSV (Pro), with nothing to set up on the server.
- PrestaShop: features, attributes, brands and category filters; classic theme menu, slider and footer links.
- WordPress: WooCommerce attributes, Elementor pages, Polylang and TranslatePress, more SEO, cache and form plugins.
- Weekly report by email, in Free and Pro.
- The history shows who approved each change.
- New panel with a Dashboard; in PrestaShop its own back-office menu.

## 2.8.1 · 2026-10-26

- The panel tells you when a new version is out.
- PrestaShop: deleting a product photo can be undone.
- Security: the encryption key is kept apart from backups; more settings hidden from the chat.

## 2.8.0 · 2026-10-26

- Connect the chat by signing in: paste the shop address in Claude or ChatGPT and click "Allow" in a window of your store.
- The Connected chats card lists every chat with its access and who approved it.

## 2.7.4 · 2026-10-17

- One package for everyone. Without a Pro licence it works as Free (reads and reports); with a Pro licence it saves changes after approval.
